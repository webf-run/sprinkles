A call-to-action section is a full-width band, usually near the end of a
page, that asks the visitor to do one thing: book a class, apply, plan a
trip. Everything in it is centred and leads to the button. For a call to
action inside the page's grid, use [CtaCard](/blocks/cta-card).

## Surface

`surface` picks the band:

| Surface   | Use it for                                                                    |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `inverse` | A dark band with a soft glow (default); buttons use their static white style. |
| `muted`   | A quiet light band, for pages that already have a dark hero or footer.        |
| `default` | The page's own surface, between tinted sections.                              |

## Accessibility

- Renders a `<section>` labelled by its title, an `<h2>`, so it's a region
  landmark with a name.
- Buttons are links (`<a>`), 48 px tall, with the focus ring on keyboard
  focus only. The arrow icon is hidden from screen readers.
- Text and buttons pass 4.5:1 contrast on every surface; on `inverse` they
  switch to white, whatever the theme's colours.
- Keep the eyebrow short: a Badge stays on one line.
