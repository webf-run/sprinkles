HeroBanner can adapt to different brands and industries through its image,
colour variant, alignment and layout. These examples show how the same
component can support different content needs without repeating one visual
composition.

## Education and community

### University with overlapping statistics

A university banner can introduce the institution and bring key facts forward.
The statistics overlap the bottom edge, creating a clear connection between
the headline and supporting information.

## Choosing a variant

The variant controls the colour of the band and the treatment of its actions.
Choose a semantic role that supports the message and the site's visual identity.

| Variant | Band | Button treatment | Common use cases |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `inverse` | Deep, the default | White with a white outline | Education, corporate |
| `positive` | Green | White with a white outline | Charity, health, nature |
| `indigo` | Deep indigo | White with a white outline | Software, technology |
| `purple` | Purple | White with a white outline | Creative, editorial, culture |
| `informative` | Blue | White with a white outline | Finance, public services |
| `neutral` | Charcoal | White with a white outline | Travel, media, portfolios |
| `highlight` | Cream with dark text | Amber with a neutral outline | Food, retail, events |
| `light` | Pale with dark text | Accent with an accent outline | Clinics, wellness, family |

Pair any variant with `imageTone='cool'` for a cooler wash over the photo.
Use `imageTone='natural'` to keep the original image treatment, or
`imageTone='warm'` for a warmer appearance, or `imageTone='purple'` for a richer purple wash.
