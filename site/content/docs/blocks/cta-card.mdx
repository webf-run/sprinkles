A CtaCard asks for an action from inside the page's grid: beside an
article, at the end of a list, or two side by side for two audiences. It's
left-aligned and sits on a muted card, so it reads as part of the content.
For a full-width band, use [CtaSection](/blocks/cta-section).

## Accessibility

- Renders a `<section>` with an `<h2>` title: the card stands at the level
  of the page's sections, not inside one.
- Buttons are links (`<a>`), 40 px tall, with the focus ring on keyboard
  focus only.
- Title and description pass 4.5:1 contrast on the muted card.
