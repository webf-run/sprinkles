A testimonial card shows what a student, a parent or a traveller said, and
who they are. Use one to back up a claim next to it, or a grid of them as a
section of their own. It shares its look with the other cards
([FeatureCard](/cards/feature-card/), [BenefitCard](/cards/benefit-card/)):
the same `variant`, `fillStyle` and `size`.

## Quote and attribution

`quote` and `name` are required; `role` says why the reader should trust
them: “Parent of a Grade 4 student”, “Travelled to Kyoto, 2026”.

## Variants

`variant` takes a tone: a semantic role (`accent` by default, `neutral`,
`informative`, `positive`, `notice`, `negative`), `highlight`, or a
categorical colour (`seafoam`, `indigo`, `purple`, `fuchsia`, `yellow`).
In the default outline style it colours the quote mark and the initials.

<ExampleBlock code={variantsCode}>
  <VariantsOutput />
</ExampleBlock>

## Fill style

`fillStyle` says how strongly the tone shows: `outline` (default) is a
surface card with a quiet border, `subtle` tints the card, and `bold` fills
it. Use `bold` for one quote that stands out, not for a whole grid.

</ExampleBlock>

## Sizes

`s`, `m` (default) and `l` set the padding, the quote's type size and the
chip. Use `l` for a single quote that carries a section.

`avatar` shows a photo in place of the initials. `rating` adds stars out of

## Accessibility

- Renders a `<figure>`: the quote in a `<blockquote>`, the person in a
  `<figcaption>`.
- The quote mark and the initials are hidden from screen readers; the photo
  has empty alt text, since the name sits beside it.
- The rating is one image named “Rated 4 out of 5”, not five separate stars.
- Quote, name and role pass 4.5:1 contrast in every tone and fill style.

## Props

| Prop        | Type                                                | Default     |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| `quote`     | `string`                                            |             |
| `name`      | `string`                                            |             |
| `role`      | `string`                                            |             |
| `avatar`    | `string`; a photo URL                               |             |
| `rating`    | `number`, 0 to 5                                    |             |
| `variant`   | a tone: a role, `highlight` or a categorical colour | `'accent'`  |
| `fillStyle` | `'outline' \| 'subtle' \| 'bold'`                   | `'outline'` |
| `size`      | `'s' \| 'm' \| 'l'`                                 | `'m'`       |
| `class`     | `string`                                            |             |
